## 3. The honest split (what is proved vs what rests on the theory)

**Kernel-checkable algebra (all proved above):** the equation's behavior at concrete
points, the x = 0 stratum, the substitution to Prop 3.6(iii), the denominator clearing,
the leaf/formula/assembly identities.

**Named gaps (not claimed in Lean):**

- **G-A (Z_(2) characterization).** `x ∈ Z_(2) ↔ Z2Def x` needs the quaternion layer:
  the ⇐ direction of Daans Lemma 4.1 is Hensel + the 2-adic parity argument (elementary
  valuations, but p-adic — not finite-domain); the ⇒ direction rests on quaternion
  splitting (ABHN class = Daans Thm 3.5 = NSW08 8.1.17). Both are XL leaves per
  LEAF_RANKING.md. The Python-side verification of the formula's use (F_2 patch soluble
  iff v₂(X) ≤ −1, and the 81/81-cell local-law table) remains machine-checked in
  `dzq-exec`, not formalized.
- **G-B (local laws as Lean theorems).** "F_p soluble over Q_p ⟺ v_p(X) ≤ −1" is stated
  in `dzq-exec`/VERIFICATION + measured cell tables; formalizing it needs a Q_p or
  valuation-ring development in Lean (Mathlib has the ingredients; not attempted in this
  stage). The finite cell-table data exists as JSON; a decide-family over it requires the
  Q_p solubility criterion in Lean first.
- **G-C (the target theorem).** "F = 0 has a rational solution ⟺ X ∉ Z" is Sun 2026
  Thm 1.1 + the Daans bridge (POLYNOMIAL.md §§2–3); the fold machinery IS formalized
  here (§1.3), the analytic chain is not.
